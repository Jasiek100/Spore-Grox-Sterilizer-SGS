OVERVIEW:
Spore Grox Sterilizer (SGS) is a standalone external utility for EA's Spore
that safely and autonomously neutralizes Grox star systems within a user-
customizable 1 to 100 parsec radius around the player spaceship.

100% VANILLA COMPATIBLE:
- NO DLL injection
- NO modification of SporeApp.exe or game binaries
- NO memory hooks or code caves
- Save games created after sterilization are 100% clean vanilla and work on
  ANY computer with an unmodded Spore install.

HOW TO USE:
1. Launch Spore and load your Space Stage save.
2. Run SGS_V1.0.exe (or Spore_Grox_Sterilizer.bat).
3. Fly your spaceship near Grox space.
4. Click "START STERILIZATION".
5. Grox systems within 20 pc are cured at 100ms intervals.
6. Fly down to any planet surface freely - terrain and oceans are fully intact.
7. Save your game normally in Spore.
